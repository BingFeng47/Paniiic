# Retro copy

Write like the 1996–1999 Web and desktop:

- Ads are loud and dishonest: ALL CAPS, too many exclamation marks, "CLICK HERE", "YOU ARE THE
  1,000,000th VISITOR!!", "FREE SCREENSAVERS", "Your PC may be infected!".
- Dialogs are dry and technical: "Verifying user name and password...", "The connection was
  terminated by the remote computer."
- Filenames use 8.3 or late-90s styles: `funny_cats_1998.avi`, `SKELETON_DATING_SIM.EXE`.
- Halloween flavour is welcome but sly. Horror should leak through the boring computer text.
- No real brands, products, people or trademarks. Invent names ("NetSurfer", "PhantomHTTPd").
- No slurs, sexual content or real-world scams aimed at the player. This is a family-friendly
  spooky game.
